Effort spent

Bars are fault groups. Faults in one group give the same readings at every test point, so no measurement can separate them. Effort points: DC reading 1, scope reading 3, a point that can exceed 50 V +2, unsoldering a part 10; budget 40.

      What this page does not show

      • The language-model layer. These replays use the offline complaint reader (rules), so the explanations Claude writes in the live app are not shown.
      • The safety workflow around a real bench: bring-up through a variac, discharge checks and removal approval happen in the app and are not replayed here.
      • Real hardware. Every reading is simulated; the 24 V fault-board test (T7) comes later.
