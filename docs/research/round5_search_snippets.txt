## Q10 (allowed_domains sound-au.com): "compare the level and sound from each channel at the same point in the circuit"
Top link: Signal Tracer For Audio Fault Finding https://sound-au.com/project164.htm
In a stereo circuit, you can compare the level and sound from each channel at the same point in the circuit. This technique is particularly useful for audio fault finding and circuit troubleshooting.
If you have a preamp, crossover network or other multi-stage circuit, you can follow the path of the signal through each individual section until you find the point where the signal stops, becomes distorted, or just sounds wrong. The stage that provides a signal that doesn't match what you expect (or hear from the other channel) is the one at fault.

## Q22 (allowed_domains sound-au.com): Elliott Sound Products "Troubleshooting & Repair Guide" test equipment ... "Variac" "lamp"
Top link: Troubleshooting & Repair Guide https://sound-au.com/troubleshooting.htm
A variable voltage transformer (Variac) allows you to make any power supply variable, and the amplifier voltage can be slowly increased while monitoring the amplifier's output voltage and supply current with a multimeter.
As an alternative to expensive Variacs, a useful test tool is a 'lamp lead' - a standard light bulb (100W incandescent is usually about right), carefully wired in series with a mains lead. An amp with a short circuit fault will cause the lamp to glow at full brightness, but a normal load will cause the lamp to flash brightly for a moment, then settle down to a steady dull glow.
