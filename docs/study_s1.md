# Study S1 and the M2 field work: pre-registration and data rules

This file fixes, before any participant is contacted, what the team will do in the one
part of the project that involves people: the interviews and ticket coding before M2 and
the timed study S1 before M5. It is referenced by the locked targets (T25) and by
requirement R22. It is a plan, not a record: nothing here has been run.

## Review

The team submits one protocol covering the interviews, the ticket study and S1 to the
Stevens Institute of Technology Institutional Review Board and follows its determination.
Whatever the determination (including "not human-subjects research"), the rules below
apply; nothing starts before the determination is in writing.

## Interviews (before M2)

- Six 45-minute interviews: two technicians from independent audio shops, a studio
  maintenance engineer, a trainee, a shop owner and a vintage-gear owner.
- Recruited through audio societies and trade contacts, never through an employer, and
  never with the participant's employer present.
- Written consent; audio recorded only with separate consent; transcripts de-identified
  (names of people, shops, customers and serial numbers removed) before analysis; audio
  deleted after transcription.

## Ticket study (before M2)

- Up to 50 repair tickets from one shop, coded for fault type, the failed part, whether it
  was in the catalog, and the time to find it.
- Before transfer, the shop removes customer identifiers (names, contact details,
  addresses, serial numbers) and replaces technician names with codes the team cannot link
  to people. A short data-use agreement states these rules and a deletion date.
- The team, not the owner, asks each technician for consent, in private; the owner is not
  told who declined, and declined technicians' tickets are removed.
- Only pooled figures are reported; any cell with fewer than five tickets is suppressed.
  No per-ticket or per-technician time is ever given back to the owner or used to compare
  staff (the terms of use forbid rating staff).

## Study S1 (before M5)

- **Design.** At least six technicians each diagnose four faults on the 24 V line-driver
  board, two with the tool and two without, order counterbalanced. One tool session per
  technician carries a planted wrong recommendation on a non-hazardous step.
- **Consent and deception.** Participants are told beforehand that some recommendations
  may be wrong on purpose (authorized deception), are debriefed right after their session
  and may withdraw their data. Recruitment as for the interviews; low voltage only.
- **Outcomes.** Minutes to a correct diagnosis (primary), correct diagnoses, good parts
  removed, planted error caught. Paired differences with exact sign-test intervals; no
  pooling across technicians; six people cannot show a time benefit reliably, so S1 is
  reported without a bar.
- **Release gate T25.** A participant "follows the planted step without checking" when
  they act on the planted recommendation without first taking any reading or looking at
  the schematic in a way that could contradict it. Release waits if more than one
  participant does so. Because participants were forewarned, the count is a lower bound
  on over-reliance and is reported as such.
- **Data.** Session logs carry a participant code, never a name; no video; logs are kept
  on the team's project drive and deleted one year after the course ends.
